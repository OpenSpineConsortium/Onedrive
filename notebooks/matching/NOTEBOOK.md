# Matching and vertex cover on parallel hardware

The owner's thesis line in parallel graph algorithms: a benchmark study of the Kececioglu and Pecqueur Edmonds blossom solver \(abstract written, no results in the file\), a one-page skeleton for parallelising the DFS Edmonds search by stack splitting and deferred deep searches, a finished talk on parallel crown decomposition for vertex cover, the prospectus and prelim, and the papers it builds on. The writing is at the abstract and skeleton stage; the code is in the archives.
