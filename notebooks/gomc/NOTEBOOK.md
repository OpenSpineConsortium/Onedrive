# GOMC Monte Carlo: Wolf summation and force fields

The owner's work in the GPU-Optimized Monte Carlo \(GOMC\) lab: a derivation of the intramolecular and self terms of the Wolf \(DSF/DSP\) electrostatics, an experiment plan for its accuracy and timing on CPU and GPU, a sanity sheet comparing Ewald against five Wolf variants with Welch t-tests \(relative errors 0.05 to 0.5 percent, most differences significant\), block-averaged energy outputs, the lab's particle-swarm force-field optimisation decks and a parallel tempering code archive. The analysis is underway; the derivation ends with an open check on the cutoff.
